# Instagram Adapter — Target Contract

## Surfaces

- public comments;
- story replies;
- private DMs.

## Public comments

Use a community/public policy:
- no private memory;
- no entitlement disclosure;
- no sensitive personal analysis;
- concise replies;
- move personal context to DM.

## DM

Resolve identity and consent before privileged context.

A social handle alone is not automatically the same identity as a WhatsApp contact.

Account linking must be explicit/verified.

## Content

The adapter may publish approved:
- cards;
- short reflections;
- challenges;
- avatar clips;
- product/sample announcements.

## Moderation

Do not expose private support context in comments.

## Analytics

Track:
- content engagement;
- inbound DM start;
- sample click;
- authorized funnel events.

Do not send private message content to generic analytics.
