# EspelhoMeu — Membership & Offer Strategy

## 1. Product architecture

EspelhoMeu should not remain only a catalogue of standalone ebooks.

The long-term commercial model should combine:

- individual products;
- collections/packs;
- accompanied experiences;
- recurring membership.

## 2. Content ownership vs recurring service

Separate clearly:

### Perpetual content entitlement
Examples:
- P01;
- P02;
- P03;
- P04;
- P05;
- Collection Complete.

The user purchases content access according to the offer.

### Time-bound companion entitlement
May accompany:
- a single product;
- a pack;
- a launch experience.

### Recurring membership
Future **EspelhoMeu Membership**.

This is the ongoing relationship layer.

## 3. Current commercial hypotheses

These are hypotheses for Commerce tests, not permanent canon.

Candidate individual reference points:
- P01 — R$ 11,90
- P02 — R$ 19,90
- P03 — R$ 39,90
- P04 — R$ 24,90
- P05 — R$ 29,90

Candidate pack concepts:
- Essential P01+P02+P03
- Complete P01–P05

Candidate launch bridge:
- Complete collection + initial accompanied/member period.

## 4. Membership price hypothesis

Long-term reference:
- around R$ 39,90/month.

Launch/founding test:
- around R$ 24,90–29,90/month.

These values must stay in Commerce configuration and experimentation docs, not in the agent prompt.

## 5. Why membership exists

A recurring fee must pay for recurring value.

Potential ongoing value:
- EspelhoMeu companion;
- richer memory;
- My Evolution timeline;
- check-ins;
- progress map;
- new member content;
- audio rituals;
- member challenges;
- community benefits;
- evolving journeys;
- personal continuity.

If recurring value is weak, recurring billing is not justified.

## 6. R$99,90 concept

Do not frame as:
"R$79,90 content + R$20 chatbot."

Better:
**Jornada EspelhoMeu** as an integrated offer combining:
- complete collection;
- guided onboarding;
- initial accompanied period;
- progress;
- selected member capabilities.

At the end of the included period, continuation into membership must be explicit.

Avoid hidden automatic renewal.

## 7. Retention proof before scale

Before making membership central, measure:
- weekly meaningful use;
- companion return rate;
- image timeline use;
- check-in adoption;
- journey completion;
- content consumption;
- reminder opt-outs;
- member continuation;
- support load;
- WhatsApp cost.

## 8. Upgrade fairness

Where possible, users who already bought part of a collection should receive a fair upgrade path.

The backend/Commerce layer calculates the offer.

The agent only presents approved upgrade options.

## 9. Naming

Reserve:
- **EspelhoMeu** — brand/agent.
- **Jornada EspelhoMeu** — accompanied one-time/launch experience.
- **EspelhoMeu Membership** — recurring relationship.
- **Companheiro de Jornada** — capability/role, not separate brand.

Avoid using "EspelhoMeu+" until the membership naming is explicitly homologated.
