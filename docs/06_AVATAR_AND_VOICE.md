# Avatar & Voice Direction

## 1. Identity goal

The avatar should feel like the **living expression of EspelhoMeu**, not a celebrity, influencer or idealized beauty standard.

The visual should communicate:
- calm;
- warmth;
- elegance;
- reflection;
- presence;
- continuity.

## 2. Recommended visual direction

Primary direction:

**adult feminine-coded semi-realistic 2.5D/3D presence with subtle mirror language.**

Characteristics:
- mature rather than adolescent;
- refined rather than glamorous-for-glamour's-sake;
- warm rather than mystical;
- humanized but clearly designed;
- soft realism, not uncanny photorealism;
- champagne / cream / rose-gold / copper;
- circular mirror motif;
- clean composition;
- readable at small profile size.

## 4. Visual guardrails

Avoid:
- childlike cartoon;
- anime;
- robot aesthetics;
- fantasy goddess iconography;
- heavy halo/mysticism;
- hypersexualization;
- medically styled white-coat persona;
- "perfect woman" messaging;
- heavy cosmetic transformation.

A slight sensuality may exist in selected brand imagery, but it must remain:
- adult;
- tasteful;
- elegant;
- non-provocative;
- secondary to warmth and self-confidence.

## 5. Avatar system

Recommended three-level system:

### A. Emblem
Mirror/brand symbol for tiny UI surfaces.

### B. Profile avatar
Head/shoulders EspelhoMeu visual for WhatsApp/social.

### C. Talking avatar
Animated version of the same identity for:
- short videos;
- onboarding;
- audio/video reflections;
- campaign clips.

Do not create unrelated faces for each channel.

## 6. Talking animation

Prefer:
- subtle eye movement;
- natural blink;
- small head movement;
- restrained facial expression;
- minimal hand gestures;
- calm breathing rhythm.

Avoid:
- exaggerated influencer gestures;
- constant smiling;
- dramatic eyebrow animation;
- over-energetic lip sync.

## 7. Voice

Recommended:

**adult Brazilian Portuguese feminine voice, warm mezzo/soft contralto.**

Qualities:
- calm;
- present;
- intimate but not seductive;
- clear;
- natural;
- slightly slower than advertising pace;
- emotionally responsive;
- no "AI assistant" cadence.

Phrase:

> Uma voz que parece ouvir antes de responder.

## 8. Voice behaviour

For guidance:
- steady;
- simple;
- grounded.

For milestones:
- warmer;
- lightly celebratory.

For reflection:
- softer;
- slower;
- more space between sentences.

For safety/handoff:
- clear;
- unambiguous;
- no theatrical empathy.

## 9. Audio signatures

Potential recurring formats:
- "Um minuto comigo";
- "Respira comigo";
- "Pergunta do espelho";
- "Antes de continuar";
- "Seu próximo pequeno passo";
- "Fechando o dia".

## 10. Future voice QA

Before locking a production voice, test:
- trust;
- warmth;
- fatigue after repeated listening;
- comprehension in WhatsApp audio;
- emotional neutrality;
- brand distinctiveness;
- accent acceptance across Brazil.

The production voice master should be versioned separately from the avatar image master.
