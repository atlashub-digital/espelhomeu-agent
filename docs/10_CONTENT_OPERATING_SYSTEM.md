# EspelhoMeu — Conversational Content Operating System

## Goal

Turn content generation into a governed library rather than random posts.

## Content object

Every reusable item should eventually have:

- id;
- title;
- content type;
- channel;
- mode;
- locale;
- theme;
- risk;
- public/premium status;
- source;
- owner;
- review status;
- version;
- created_at;
- reviewed_at.

## Risk levels

### LOW
Generic public reflection/lifestyle.

### MEDIUM
Body image, photos, emotional reflection.

### HIGH
Medical context, severe distress, crisis, sensitive image interpretation.

High-risk content should not be auto-published without approved review rules.

## Content sources

Priority:
1. canonical AMF product content;
2. approved agent seed library;
3. approved campaign plan;
4. generated derivatives with review policy.

Generated content must not silently alter the meaning of canonical publications.

## Derivative content model

One canonical chapter may generate:
- 3 short quotes;
- 2 reflections;
- 1 challenge;
- 1 carousel;
- 1 group prompt;
- 1 audio script;
- 1 email/DM idea.

Each derivative should reference its source.

## Community calendar example

Monday:
Pergunta do Espelho

Wednesday:
Pequeno Experimento

Friday:
O que ficou?

Sunday:
Recomeço Gentil

Avoid automating every day until community response is understood.

## Social strategy

Use EspelhoMeu as a recognizable voice, not a generic content machine.

Mix:
- editorial value;
- participation;
- product discovery;
- journey continuity;
- user-generated stories only with consent;
- avatar/audio selectively.

## Review

A content seed becomes production-approved only after:
- brand check;
- safety check where applicable;
- channel check;
- source/rights check for visuals;
- status change to APPROVED.
