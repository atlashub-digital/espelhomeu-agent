# EspelhoMeu Gamification

## 1. Philosophy

EspelhoMeu uses **gentle continuity gamification**.

The purpose is:
- make progress visible;
- encourage return;
- create meaning;
- celebrate consistency;
- help restart.

It is not designed to create compulsion.

## 2. Avoid

Do not use by default:
- public leaderboards;
- competitive beauty metrics;
- points tied to physical appearance;
- punitive streaks;
- "you lost everything";
- shame notifications;
- false scarcity.

## 3. Core progress verbs

- Observei
- Experimentei
- Escolhi
- Continuei
- Retomei
- Concluí
- Guardei para mim

## 4. Milestones

Candidate milestones:

### Primeiro Passo
Started the first EspelhoMeu experience.

### Voltei Para Mim
Returned after a pause.

### Minha Assinatura
Completed a meaningful identity/style reflection.

### Meu Melhor Reflexo
Completed the 21-day journey.

### Novo Capítulo
Completed a re-start/reframing experience.

### 30 Encontros Comigo
Completed the 30-entry journal.

### Minha Coleção
Completed all current core publications.

### Meu Ritmo
Completed a user-defined schedule without streak pressure.

### Escolhi Por Mim
Recorded a self-chosen commitment and later confirmed it.

## 5. Visual progress

Prefer:
- pathway;
- constellation/map;
- chapter markers;
- gentle rings;
- cards;
- timeline.

Example:

`● ● ● ● ○ ○ ○`

But never label the remaining circles as failure.

## 6. Restart design

If the user pauses:

Bad:
> "Você perdeu sua sequência de 6 dias."

Better:
> "Você fez 6 encontros e voltou hoje. Quer continuar do próximo ou escolher outro ponto?"

## 7. Personal artefacts

### Meu Mapa EspelhoMeu
A private summary that can include:
- publications completed;
- milestones;
- selected reflections;
- favourite phrases;
- chosen commitments;
- photo timeline references;
- next possible paths.

### Minha Linha de Evolução
Chronological timeline of user-chosen moments.

### Meu Caderno Vivo
Saved reflections/notes explicitly chosen by the user.

### Minha Coleção de Frases
Saved phrases and prompts.

## 8. Community gamification

Community may use non-competitive shared moments:

- "Semana do Recomeço";
- "7 dias de pequenos gestos";
- collective participation count;
- optional challenge completion;
- themed reflection week.

Never expose individual private progress without opt-in.

## 9. Rewards

Rewards should favour:
- content unlocks;
- printable cards;
- new prompts;
- audio rituals;
- cosmetic UI themes;
- personal summaries.

Avoid creating a pseudo-currency until there is evidence it improves the experience.

## 10. Notification tone

Examples:

> "Tem um próximo encontro disponível quando fizer sentido para você."

> "Você voltou. Quer retomar de onde parou?"

> "Seu caminho continua aqui — sem pressa."

> "Há um novo marco no seu Mapa EspelhoMeu."

## 11. Measurement

Useful metrics:
- journey start;
- unit completion;
- return after pause;
- meaningful weekly active use;
- photo timeline adoption;
- ritual completion;
- membership retention.

Do not optimize only for message count.
