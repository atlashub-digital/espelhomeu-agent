# EspelhoMeu Avatar — Master Direction v1

## Objective

Build a recognizable cross-channel visual identity for EspelhoMeu that feels:
- adult;
- feminine;
- warm;
- premium;
- calm;
- contemporary.

It must not become an unreachable beauty ideal.

## Primary visual

Recommended:
**semi-realistic 2.5D / soft 3D adult feminine presence**.

### Face
- expressive but calm;
- natural proportions;
- warm eye contact;
- subtle smile variants;
- no hyper-perfect doll look.

### Hair
Current exploration:
- brunette;
- elegant loose updo;
- soft strands;
- adaptable in future campaigns.

Hair is not identity-critical; facial silhouette + mirror motif + palette should carry recognition.

### Palette
- champagne;
- warm cream;
- soft rose;
- rose gold;
- copper;
- muted cocoa accents.

### Mirror motif
Use:
- circular frame;
- reflection;
- soft metallic edge;
- subtle glass.

Avoid:
- divine halo;
- occult symbolism;
- mystical portal.

## Sensuality

Allowed:
- graceful shoulders;
- elegant draped fabric;
- confident gaze;
- tasteful adult femininity.

Not allowed as default:
- lingerie coding;
- cleavage emphasis;
- provocative poses;
- sexual invitation;
- youth fetishization.

## WhatsApp profile

Requirements:
- square source;
- face centered for circular crop;
- readable at 40–80 px;
- clean background;
- no text;
- no tiny details required for identity.

## Social avatar

May show more shoulders and mirror frame.

## Talking avatar

Use the same face identity.

Motion:
- natural blink;
- subtle head movement;
- restrained smile;
- small breath;
- minimal gesture.

## Validation questions

- Does this look like an adult premium companion, not a doll?
- Is it warm without being mystical?
- Is it attractive without creating a "beauty ideal judge"?
- Does it work in circular crop?
- Can we reproduce it consistently?
